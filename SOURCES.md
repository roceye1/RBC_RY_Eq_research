## Market-data timing and reconciliation

- USD market price for the valuation benchmark: **RY US$203.76 at the 5 November 2026 close**. All percentage returns in the scenario model are relative to this benchmark, not to the previous day's US$209.79 price.
- The future-period consensus snapshot used here shows **FY2026E revenue C$72.42B**, **FY2027E revenue C$76.44B**, **FY2026E adjusted/normalized EPS C$16.42**, and **FY2027E adjusted/normalized EPS C$17.91**. Treat these as time-stamped estimates, not audited reported results.
- The USD conversion of forecast EPS uses **US$0.723 per C$1 solely as an illustrative factor**, not an observed current forward FX quote.
- Forward-P/E figures on slide 13 are public vendor snapshots. They can differ from proprietary Bloomberg fields and may change with intraday price/earnings estimate updates.
- FY2023–2025 historical reporting is used to avoid non-comparable pre-IFRS-17 FY2022/FY2023 comparisons.
